# ClipForge update channel

The ClipForge app downloads its updates from this folder. Every file is checked against ClipForge's signature and SHA-256 before anything is installed.

Current version: **1.7.0**

- **Scripts are planned before they're written:** what the viewer learns, the angle, the order, and what to leave out.
- **Hooks:** 6 are written and the strongest is chosen. Generic openers ("Did you know…", "Here are…") are rejected.
- **Every script is reviewed before it's voiced.** The review scores hook, information, accuracy, flow, clarity, natural speech, relevance and pacing. Weak scripts are rewritten. If one still isn't good enough, ClipForge **doesn't make the video** and shows you why, plus its best attempt.
- **No invented GTA facts:** payouts, locations and mechanics must come from the GTA Wiki pages it looks up.
- **The AI production brief no longer changes what your video says**, unless you edit it yourself.
- **Hybrid videos keep enough time for the narration.**
